Bane privacy policy

Bane is a private assistant used by a single household. It is not offered to the public.

When a household member signs in with Google, Bane uses the access they approve (such as calendar and email) only to help that household. That data stays on the household's own computer. It is never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Any household member can remove Bane's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, Third-party apps.

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
